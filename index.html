Features, instructions & important notes

Meet your wardrobe again.

Turn the clothes you already own into fresh outfit ideas. AI My Wardrobe brings a photo-first closet, everyday combinations and optional AI try-on into one clean iPhone app.

YOUR CLOTHES, CLEARLY ORGANIZED
Take a photo or choose one from Photos. On-device processing prepares a tidy catalog image with the goal of keeping the full garment visible. Add its name, category and color, search your collection and tap a photo to see it larger.

IDEAS FOR THE DAY AHEAD
Explore office, weekend and outdoor combinations. Refresh for another look and save favorites. Optional local weather adds temperature context, with units following your iPhone region. Start instantly with three demo outfits. Basic wardrobe features work without an API key.

CHOOSE YOUR AI
Connect your own OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen or compatible HTTPS API for conversational styling. Supported OpenAI and Google Gemini image models can create visual try-on images from your chosen clothes and model photo. Model access depends on your provider and account.

YOUR REFERENCE MODEL
Choose fictional male or female AI models that follow your language and region, or choose a region manually. Add optional front, left, right and back photos to use your own appearance.

GET STARTED
1. Explore the demo wardrobe or add clothing in Closet.
2. Choose an occasion in Today, refresh and save a look.
3. For online AI, open Me → AI settings, enter your own key and model, review data-sharing details, explicitly allow sharing and save.
4. In Try on, choose or photograph a garment and tap AI image. Add the garment to your closet when you want.

KNOW BEFORE YOU START
The app is free. Online AI is optional and requires your own API key, internet access and a compatible account. Provider charges may apply, including connection tests. API access is not sold in the app. AI images are styling approximations, not a fit, size or measurement guarantee. Camera and location permissions are optional. Photos and conversations are sent to your chosen AI provider only after explicit permission, which you can revoke. Your wardrobe is stored locally, without cloud sync or app-data backup in this edition; authorized AI requests are processed by your provider. Delete individual items or all local data and keys in Me.

English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, German and Spanish. Designed for portrait use on iPhone.

VOICE INPUT, YOUR CHOICE
Tap the microphone to dictate in your app language. Review or edit the draft, then send when ready. Microphone and Apple speech permissions are optional. On-device recognition is used where available; otherwise Apple may process audio. The app does not save voice recordings.

Today shows a dressed default model with an offline outfit sketch. Refresh changes the selected combination without paid AI calls. Only tapping AI image requests online image generation. Offline sketches are approximate and may not reproduce garment fit accurately.

Privacy policy

AI My Wardrobe stores clothing photos, optional body photos, saved looks and conversations on your iPhone. No account, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK is used. The developer operates no content server. App data is excluded from cloud backup; deleting the app may permanently remove it. API keys are stored in the device Keychain and may survive app removal, so delete keys in Settings before uninstalling.

AI features are optional. After your explicit permission, outfit requests send garment names, categories, colors, recent messages, language and temperature to your selected provider: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen or your custom HTTPS endpoint. Image generation additionally sends your chosen model or front body photo and clothing photos to OpenAI or Google Gemini. Your face and body may be visible. Keys authenticate directly with that provider. No body measurements, nationality or health traits are inferred.

Providers process requests under their own terms, security practices and retention policies, potentially outside your country. Review the links below before enabling sharing. For a custom endpoint, verify its operator and privacy policy yourself. Permissions are off by default and can be revoked in AI settings; revocation stops future requests but cannot retract data already transmitted.

Camera access is requested only when taking photos. The system photo picker provides only photos you select. Optional approximate location goes to Apple Weather for local weather; coordinates are not sent to AI providers. You can decline these permissions and keep using the wardrobe.

Delete individual garments or body photos, or use “Delete all local data” to remove clothing, photos, looks, conversations, settings and saved API keys. To delete provider-held data, contact that provider. No app account exists to delete.

For privacy requests, use the support page below. Do not post photos, keys or sensitive information in public issues. This policy may be updated with releases; review it before consenting to new processing.

Voice input is optional. Microphone and Apple speech-recognition permissions are requested when you tap the button. Recognition follows the app language and runs on device when available; otherwise Apple may process audio. Recordings are not saved or sent to AI providers. Transcripts remain editable until you tap Send. Stopping or leaving the screen ends microphone capture.

Optional height and weight are stored only on this device and are not included in AI requests. You can edit or clear them in Me; Delete all local data also removes them.
